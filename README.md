# BUE Civil Engineering Projects

Home page for the interactive recruitment projects of the Department of Civil Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, The British University in Egypt.

**Live page:** https://bue-civil.github.io/

| Project | What it is | Link |
|---|---|---|
| STRUCTURA 3D | Build a steel frame and test it against wind and earthquakes with a real 3D stiffness analysis. Phones, tablets and computers. | https://bue-civil.github.io/structura-3d/ |
| Cardington VR | Walk through the eight-storey Cardington steel test frame, with facades, interiors, lifts and a fire drill. Opens in VR on a Meta Quest. | https://bue-civil.github.io/cardington-vr/ |

Single `index.html`, no build step. Logos are in `assets/`.
