STRUCTURA 3D

Build a steel frame from columns, beams, bracing, shear walls and floors, keep to a budget, then test it against wind and earthquakes. A real 3D stiffness analysis shows your building sway, twist, or collapse.

Cardington VR

Explore the eight-storey Cardington steel test frame at full scale. Add glass facades and office interiors, walk the floors, ride the lifts, and run a fire drill that evacuates the whole building by its stairs. Opens in VR on a Meta Quest.
